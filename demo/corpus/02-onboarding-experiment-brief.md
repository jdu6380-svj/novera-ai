# Asteria Workcloud — Guided Onboarding Experiment Brief

## Decision context

The Q2 retention review identified a drop in first-week activation and a concentration of setup questions. This brief defines a bounded experiment for new RelayOS trial workspaces. It is a product experiment, not a promise that retention will improve.

## Experiment design

Between June 10 and June 24, 2026, 420 eligible trial workspaces were randomly assigned to a control group or a guided onboarding group, 210 workspaces per group.

The guided flow presents three ordered actions: create a first workflow, invite one teammate, and connect an integration. It also explains that a sandbox workspace can skip integration setup and return later.

The primary success event is publishing a first workflow within 48 hours. Secondary events are connecting an integration within 48 hours and requesting onboarding help.

## Observed results

The guided flow increased first-workflow publication within 48 hours from 51% in control to 67% in treatment, a 16 percentage-point lift.

Integration connection within 48 hours increased from 38% in control to 49% in treatment.

Onboarding help requests fell from 28 to 17 per 100 workspaces in the guided group.

The sample is not mature enough to claim a 30-day or 90-day retention effect. Retention should remain a follow-up metric after the cohort completes the observation window.

## Rollout recommendation

Roll out the guided flow to 50% of new trials starting July 15, 2026, while retaining a control holdout. Review first-workflow publication weekly and review 30-day retention after the cohort matures.

Growth Product owns the rollout. Customer Success owns qualitative follow-up. The guardrails are trial-to-paid conversion, integration error rate, and support volume; a regression in any guardrail pauses the rollout for review.

## Open questions

The experiment does not identify which wording change drove the lift. A follow-up test should isolate role-mapping copy from the integration skip affordance. No conclusion should be drawn about ARR or customer-level profitability from this brief.
